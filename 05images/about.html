About

Maple & Moss started as a folding table and a borrowed espresso machine at a college board game night. Word got around, the table got bigger, and eventually the games moved out of a dorm lounge and into a real space with real chairs. The name comes from two things we couldn't design a café without: maple, for the syrup in our fall drinks and the wood on every tabletop, and moss, for the slow, unhurried pace we wanted the place to have.

We're still small on purpose. No drive-thru, no rush — just good coffee, a well-stocked game shelf, and enough couch space for a full party to lose a round of something together.

Fan Favorites

These are the drinks and snacks our regulars order the most, ranked by how often they show up on the counter:

  1. Maple Oat Latte — our signature, made with real maple syrup, not flavoring
  2. Moss Green Tea Cold Brew — steeped 18 hours, served over ice
  3. Cardamom Cinnamon Roll — baked fresh every morning, gone by noon
  4. Drip Coffee, rotating single-origin
  5. Hot Chocolate with house-made marshmallow

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy something to play the games?
Nope. The game shelf is free to browse and play from as long as we're open, though we always appreciate the business.
Can I bring my own board game or console?
Absolutely — just check in with the counter first if it needs to hook up to one of our TVs, since we run a rotation during busy hours.
Is Tuesday game night open to everyone?
Yes, no sign-up required. Just show up by 6pm if you want a seat at one of the bigger tables.
Do you host private events?
We do. Reach out ahead of time and we can set aside the back room for birthdays, tournaments, or study groups.
