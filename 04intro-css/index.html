Home

Maple & Moss is a small café built for slow mornings and long afternoons. Every table has room for a deck of cards, a controller, or just your laptop and a good playlist.

We opened because we couldn't find a place that felt like both a coffee shop and a living room. So we built one. Grab a window seat, borrow a board game from the shelf, or settle into a corner with one of our retro consoles hooked up and ready to go.

What's on the Shelf?

Every table is within reach of something to play. Our library grows every month, mostly from customer requests.

  • A rotating shelf of 40+ board and card games, free to borrow while you're here
  • Two retro console stations (NES and N64) with a couch built for co-op
  • A Nintendo Switch corner, complete with a small speaker so you can call with your friends
  • Weekly Tuesday game nights, first round of coffee on the house
  • Quiet corners with outlets for anyone who just needs to get work done

Visit Us

We're a short walk from Main Street and we are open every day but Monday. Doors open at 7am for the early birds, and we keep pulling shots until 9pm most nights.
